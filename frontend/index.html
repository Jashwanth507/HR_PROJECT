FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the JK Private Limited recruitment process.

How long does the aptitude test take?

The aptitude test consists of 30 randomized multiple-choice questions and has a strict time limit of 30 minutes. Once you start, a countdown timer will display, and the test will auto-submit when the time expires.

Do I need to install any tracking software?

No, there is no software to install. The secure testing portal runs entirely in your web browser. It features tab-focus monitoring and web-cam verification (simulated) to ensure anti-cheat compliance.

What is the passing score to qualify for interviews?

You need to score 70% or higher (correctly answer at least 21 out of 30 questions) to qualify. Upon submitting the test, your score is evaluated instantly, and a scorecard is generated immediately on your dashboard.

How do I book my interview slot if I qualify?

Qualified candidates will see an active "Schedule Interview" panel on their Candidate Dashboard. You can browse available live slots set by our HR panel and book your interview instantly with a single click.

Can I upload my resume after registration?

Yes. During registration or at any time on your dashboard, you can upload your resume (PDF format, up to 5MB) using our drag-and-drop zone. You can preview or update your resume whenever needed.
